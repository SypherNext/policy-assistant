# Sick Leave

## Telling us you are ill
If you are too ill to work, tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day, by phone or chat. Record the absence in the HR portal on the day you return.

## Paid sick days
You get 10 fully paid sick days per calendar year. After that, sick pay drops to 70 percent of your base salary for up to 6 months.

## Doctor's note
You can self-certify up to 3 consecutive days. From the 4th consecutive day of absence you need a note from a doctor, uploaded to the HR portal.

## Caring for a sick child
Caring for a sick child under 12 counts as sick leave for up to 5 days a year.
