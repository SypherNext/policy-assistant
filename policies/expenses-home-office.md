# Home Office Equipment Allowance

Employees with a remote or hybrid contract can claim a one-off allowance of up to 400 EUR for home office equipment, such as a chair, a desk, or a lamp. Claim it in the Expenses app with receipts within your first 6 months. Larkfield provides the laptop and one external monitor separately.
