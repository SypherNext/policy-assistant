# Data Classification

Every document and data set at Larkfield has one of four classification levels. The owner of the data chooses the level when the data is created and reviews it once a year.

## Public
Information meant for anyone outside the company, such as the website, published blog posts, and job adverts. Public data can be shared freely.

## Internal
Information for employees and contractors, such as the staff handbook, internal announcements, and most project documents. Internal data must not be posted on public sites, but you can share it inside the company without asking.

## Confidential
Information that would harm Larkfield or a customer if it leaked: customer data, contracts, financial forecasts, and source code. Share Confidential data only with people who need it for their work, store it only in approved company systems, and never send it to a personal email address.

## Restricted
The most sensitive data: salary data, health information, passwords and keys, and security incident reports. Access is granted by name, logged, and reviewed every quarter. Restricted data must never leave approved systems, and it must never be printed.

## Labels
Label Confidential and Restricted documents in the header or the file name. If a document has no label, treat it as Internal until the owner says otherwise.
