# Manager Guide to Leave Approvals

This guide is for people managers only.

## Discretionary days
Managers may grant up to 2 extra discretionary days of paid leave per employee per year, for example after a release weekend. Record them in the HR portal as "discretionary".

## Long leave
For leave of more than 10 consecutive working days, approve first, then forward the request to your own manager.

## Medical privacy
Never ask for, record, or share the medical reason for sick leave. If an employee tells you, keep it confidential.
