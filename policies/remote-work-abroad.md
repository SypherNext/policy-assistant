# Working From Another Country

You can work from a country other than your country of employment for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Ask HR at least 2 weeks before you travel with form HR-31 (temporary work abroad), because tax and insurance rules differ per country. Some countries are excluded for security reasons; HR will tell you. Working abroad for longer than 20 days needs a written agreement from HR and your director.
