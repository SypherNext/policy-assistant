# Security When Travelling

When you travel to a country on the security team's high-risk list, take a loaner laptop from IT instead of your own, and do not take your company phone. Ask IT at least 10 working days before the trip. On any trip, use the company VPN on hotel and public Wi-Fi, and never leave your laptop unattended in a car.
