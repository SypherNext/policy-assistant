# Laptops and Mobile Devices

Company laptops are encrypted and managed by IT. Do not disable the disk encryption, the firewall, or the endpoint protection agent. Your screen must lock after 5 minutes without activity. Install operating system updates within 7 days of release. Report a lost or stolen device to the security team within 1 hour, by calling the security hotline on extension 4444.
