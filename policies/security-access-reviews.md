# Manager Guide to Access Reviews

This guide is for people managers only.

Every quarter, managers review which systems each person in their team can access, in the access review tool. Remove access that is no longer needed within 5 working days. When someone leaves your team, request removal of their access on their last day. Access to Restricted data must be renewed by name every quarter.
