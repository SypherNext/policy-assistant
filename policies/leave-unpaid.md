# Unpaid Leave and Sabbaticals

After 2 years of service you can request up to 3 months of unpaid leave (a sabbatical). Requests go to your director at least 3 months before the start date. Your job is held for you, and your benefits pause during the sabbatical. Shorter unpaid leave of up to 10 days needs only your manager's approval.
