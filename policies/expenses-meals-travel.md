# Meals While Travelling

When a business trip includes an overnight stay, you can claim up to 60 EUR per day for meals. On a day trip with no overnight stay, the limit is 25 EUR. Alcohol is not reimbursed. Tips are included in the daily limit. Meals provided by a conference or a client do not count towards the limit and cannot be claimed.
