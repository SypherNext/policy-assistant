# Public Holidays and Holiday Swaps

Larkfield observes the public holidays of the country in your employment contract. You may swap up to 2 public holidays per year for other days that matter to you, for example a religious or cultural holiday. Request the swap in the HR portal at least 4 weeks before the public holiday you want to work.
