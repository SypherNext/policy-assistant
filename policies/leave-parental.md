# Parental Leave

## Primary caregiver
The primary caregiver of a new child, by birth, adoption or surrogacy, gets 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, to be taken within the first 12 months.

## Secondary caregiver
The secondary caregiver gets 6 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be split into up to three blocks within the first 12 months.

## How to apply
Tell HR at least 8 weeks before the expected start date using form HR-12 (parental leave notice). Annual leave keeps accruing during parental leave.
