# Business Travel FAQ

## Who books my travel?
Book flights, trains, and hotels through the travel desk in the Expenses app. Bookings you make yourself are only reimbursed if the travel desk could not offer a fare within 10 percent of yours.

## Can I fly business class?
Economy class is the default. Business class is allowed for flights longer than 6 hours.

## How much can I spend on food when I travel?
For trips with an overnight stay you can spend up to 60 EUR per day on meals. Day trips are capped at 25 EUR. Alcohol is never covered.

## How much can I spend on a hotel?
Up to 180 EUR per night, or up to 250 EUR per night in London, Paris, New York, Zurich, and Tokyo.

## Can I extend a trip for a holiday?
Yes, if the extra days do not raise the cost of the trip. You pay for the extra nights yourself and book them as annual leave.
