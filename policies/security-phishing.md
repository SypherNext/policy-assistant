# Phishing and Suspicious Messages

If an email or chat message asks you to click a link, open an attachment, pay an invoice, or share a code, and anything about it feels unusual, report it with the "Report phishing" button in your mail client. Do not forward it to colleagues. If you already clicked a link or entered a password, call the security hotline on extension 4444 straight away and change the password. Nobody is ever punished for reporting a mistake.
