# Annual Leave (2023 edition)

## Entitlement
Every full-time employee gets 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays. Part-time employees get a pro-rata entitlement.

## Booking leave
Book leave in the HR portal at least 4 weeks in advance. Your manager approves or declines within 5 working days.

## Carrying days over
You may carry up to 3 unused days into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March.
