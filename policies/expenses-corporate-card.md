# Corporate Cards

Employees who travel for work more than 4 times a year can request a corporate card from finance. The card is for business costs only; personal use is not allowed, even if you pay it back. Upload receipts for card payments in the Expenses app within 30 days. A lost card must be reported to finance and the card provider the same day.
