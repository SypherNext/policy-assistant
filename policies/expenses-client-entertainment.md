# Client Entertainment

You can spend up to 80 EUR per person on meals or events with clients, including yourself. Any event with a total cost above 500 EUR needs pre-approval with form EXP-7. Record the names of the guests and their company on the claim. Gifts to clients are limited to 50 EUR per person per year.
