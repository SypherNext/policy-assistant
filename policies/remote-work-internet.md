# Internet and Phone Allowance

Fully remote employees receive an internet allowance of 30 EUR per month, paid automatically with salary. Hybrid and office-based employees do not get the allowance. Larkfield does not pay for personal mobile phone contracts; employees who are on call get a company phone.
