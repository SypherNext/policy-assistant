# Annual Leave

## Entitlement
Every full-time employee at Larkfield gets 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays. Part-time employees get the same entitlement in proportion to their contracted hours: someone working 3 days a week gets 15 days.

## Booking leave
Book leave in the HR portal at least 2 weeks before the first day off. Your manager approves or declines within 3 working days. Leave of more than 10 consecutive working days needs approval from your manager's manager as well.

## Carrying days over
You may carry up to 5 unused days into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, after which they expire. Days above the limit of 5 are lost on 1 January unless HR agreed an exception in writing.

## Leaving the company
When you leave, unused leave for the current year is paid out in your final salary. Leave you took beyond your pro-rata entitlement is deducted.
