# Remote and Hybrid Work

Larkfield has three working patterns. Your contract says which one applies to you.

## Office-based
You work from the office. You may work from home for up to 1 day a week, agreed with your manager in advance.

## Hybrid
You work from home for up to 3 days a week and from the office on the other days. Your team agrees on at least 1 shared office day each week so that people meet in person. Swapping a day is fine when your manager agrees.

## Fully remote
You work from home or another approved place in your country of employment. You come to an office or team meeting at least once a quarter; travel for these meetings is paid under the expenses policy.

## Core hours
Whatever your pattern, be available between 10:00 and 15:00 in your local time zone on working days. Outside core hours you can plan your own time, as long as your team knows how to reach you.

## Your workspace at home
Your home workspace must have a stable internet connection, a door or quiet corner for calls, and a screen that people walking past cannot read. Lock your laptop when you leave it, even at home.

## Changing your pattern
To change your working pattern, ask your manager and HR. Changes start at the beginning of a month, and HR confirms them in writing.
