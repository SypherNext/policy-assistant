# Reporting a Security Incident

A security incident is anything that may expose company or customer data: a lost laptop, a password typed into a fake site, a file shared with the wrong person, or malware. Report it within 1 hour of noticing it, by calling the security hotline on extension 4444 or with the incident form IS-3 in the IT portal. Do not try to investigate or clean up on your own.
