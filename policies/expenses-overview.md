# Expenses Policy Overview

## What you can claim
You can claim costs you pay personally while doing your job: travel, meals on business trips, client entertainment, and approved equipment.

## How to claim
Submit every claim in the Expenses app within 30 days of the purchase. Attach an itemised receipt for any amount above 25 EUR. Claims submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed.

## When you are paid
Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary run. Claims approved after the 20th of the month are paid the month after.

## Exceptions
If a cost will go above a limit in this policy, ask before you spend, using form EXP-7 (expense exception request).
