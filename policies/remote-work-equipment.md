# Equipment for Remote Workers

Remote and hybrid employees get a laptop and one external monitor from IT. On top of that you can claim a one-off home office allowance of up to 400 EUR for furniture and accessories, such as a desk, a chair, or a headset. Submit the receipts in the Expenses app within 6 months of starting. Return all IT equipment when you leave the company.
