# Bereavement Leave

If a close family member dies (partner, child, parent, sibling, or grandparent), you get 5 days of paid bereavement leave. For other relatives or close friends you get 2 days. Tell your manager as soon as you can; you do not need to provide documents. Days do not need to be consecutive and can be used within 3 months.
