# Hotels and Accommodation

The nightly hotel limit is 180 EUR, including taxes and breakfast. In high-cost cities (London, Paris, New York, Zurich, and Tokyo) the limit is 250 EUR. If you stay with friends or family instead of a hotel, you can claim a flat 30 EUR per night. Book through the travel desk where possible.
