# Manager Guide to Expense Exceptions

This guide is for people managers only.

Managers may approve an EXP-7 exception of up to 200 EUR above a policy limit per claim without further sign-off. Anything above that goes to the finance director. Team budget for team events is 45 EUR per person per quarter. Do not approve your own claims; they go to your manager.
